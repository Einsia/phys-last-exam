Photorealistic plane-mirror optics experiment on a matte laboratory table. A rigid vertical rectangular mirror stands exactly upright along a clearly visible base edge. One tall narrow red cuboid with a distinctive two-color band is positioned fully in front of the mirror, and its complete upright reflection is sharp and unobstructed. A thin laser ray from a low source strikes the mirror at an oblique angle and forms a visible reflected ray on the tabletop. Use a high oblique camera so the real object, mirror plane, virtual image appearance, object height, and both ray segments can all be measured in the same frame.
