Photorealistic studio shot looking down at a steep oblique angle onto a large, flat, pale matte grey floor panel that fills the frame. A single small bare white bulb on a slim stand stands upright at the centre of the panel, close to the floor, and is the only light in the scene. Each rod casts one long, crisp, unmistakably dark shadow stretching radially outward away from the bulb across the pale floor, and each of the four shadows is separate from the others along its whole length. The floor is bright enough that the shadows read as strongly darker than the floor beside them. Every rod, every rod base and every complete shadow is fully visible at once. No second light source, no coloured gels, no overlapping shadows, no other objects, plain background beyond the panel.
